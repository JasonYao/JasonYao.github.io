## AT LAST, SIR TERRY, WE MUST WALK TOGETHER. 

I loved reading growing up, with some of my favorites genres being
sci-fi (Orson Scott Card, Pierce Brown), or general fantasy (Terry
Pratchett, Brandon Sanderson).

My copy of Good Omens is pretty well-worn at this point, essentially
falling apart, but it's one that still means a lot to me even now over
15 years+ from when I first read it, and I can't wait to introduce my
future kids to his writing, and hopefully pass along that same love of
reading.

## GNU Terry Pratchett

> "A man is not dead while his name is still spoken." - Grandad

In 2015, Sir Terry Pratchett died from complications from his fight with
Alzheimer's disease.

If you inspect the responses for this website, you'll find that all
HTTP responses for this site end up returning a weird, strange, non-standard
response header:

```
X-Clacks-Overhead
```

This response header is deliberate, and is a living, continuous tribute to
somebody who managed to impart to me a love of reading, of imagining new
worlds, and of seeing wonders through books.

And not to be too morbid, but if I ever get to the point where I too pass,
then it'll be nice to know that somewhere around the internet, passing through
the internet Clacks will be `GNU Terry Pratchett, Jason Yao`.

## Origin

[X-Clacks-Overhead](https://xclacksoverhead.org/home/about) is a non-standardised HTTP
header based upon the fictional work of the late, great, Sir Terry Pratchett.

In Terry Pratchett's science-fantasy Discworld series, [The Clacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology_of_the_Discworld#The_clacks)
is a network infrastructure of Semaphore Towers, that operate in a similar fashion to
telegraph - named "Clacks" because of the clicking sound the system makes as signals send.

In Sir Terry's novel "Going Postal", the story explains that the inventor of the Clacks -
a man named Robert Dearheart, lost his only son in a suspicious workplace accident, and in
order to keep the memory of his son alive, he transmitted his son's name as a special
operational signal through the Clacks to forever preserve his memory:

```
GNU John Dearheart
```

Where the message's shortcode prefix commands a given Clacks Tower to:

* G: Send the message onto the next Clacks Tower.
* N: Do not log the message.
* U: At the end of the line, return the message.

The nature of the `GNU` code would keep the name of his son forever transmitting through
The Clacks network so long as the network still existed.

Terry Pratchett sadly passed away in March 2015. The phrase `GNU Terry Pratchett` has become
a term used by fans to mourn and memorialise him. As well as being written by fans in messages,
it has also been embedded in the infrastructure of the internet as a tribute, inspired by its
origins in Going Postal.

This website has also been added to XClacksOverhead.org, which aggregates sites that
continue passing Terry Pratchett's name across the internet.
